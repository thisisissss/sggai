04 — For Growers

Cannabis on aquaponics. Proven outdoors.

Thailand's cannabis market has inverted since legalization: oversupply pushed flower prices down while indoor growers still carry industrial electricity bills. We design and install outdoor aquaponic systems that remove the two biggest cost lines — energy and fertilizer — and add a second harvest from the same water: fish. The approach fits both medical-grade flower and biomass grown for industrial material.

~50% of indoor operating cost is typically energy — lighting, HVAC, dehumidification
0 ฿ spent on fertilizer — the fish and the biofilter produce the nutrients
3–4 m plant height reached in our outdoor aquaponic beds under natural sun
2 revenue streams from one system — biomass and market fish
Medical Cannabis

Clean input, stable chemistry

Medical-grade cultivation lives or dies on consistency and a clean profile. A closed-loop aquaponic bed with monitored water chemistry delivers exactly that — no synthetic fertilizer salts, no heavy-metal-laden soil amendments, and a continuously stable root zone.

  • No synthetic nutrient salts entering the plant
  • pH / EC / DO / ORP logged 24/7 for traceable, repeatable batches
  • Organic nitrate supply reduces the residue risk in lab testing
  • Stable conditions support consistent cannabinoid expression
Material / Industrial

Maximum biomass per baht

For fibre, hurd, seed, or extraction feedstock the goal is raw tonnage at the lowest input cost. Unrestricted roots and full sun push plants to multi-metre frames, while sunlight and the biofilter carry the energy and nutrient load that would otherwise dominate the budget.

  • Free solar energy replaces the entire lighting bill
  • Large frames and dense canopy maximise total dry biomass
  • Zero fertilizer cost across a high-volume crop
  • Scales across modular grow beds on open land

The biology is straightforward: fish excrete ammonia, nitrifying bacteria in the biofilter convert it to nitrate, and plant roots absorb it directly from continuously circulating water. It is the same nitrogen cycle a fertilizer schedule tries to imitate — except it runs 24 hours a day at zero marginal cost, with no salt accumulation and no nutrient lockout.

Combine that with unrestricted root zones and full tropical sun, and the plant expresses its actual genetic potential: multi-meter frames carrying dozens of flowering sites — a scale of growth an indoor tent physically cannot accommodate. No lamps, no air conditioning, no nutrient bottles.

  • Sunlight replaces grow lights — the largest electricity line item disappears
  • Biofilter replaces the fertilizer schedule — continuous plant-available nitrate
  • pH / EC / DO / ORP sensors keep water chemistry stable through every growth stage
  • The same water raises market fish — protein or ornamental sales on top of the crop
  • We design, build, and monitor the system on your site
