01 — Ecosystem

A living system,
already producing.

Our aquaponics facility is not a concept — it is an operational farm generating real harvests today. Fish waste feeds plants. Plants purify water. Sensors monitor every variable, every hour.

  • IBC tank infrastructure — modular, HDPE-grade, fully scalable
  • Multi-stage grow beds with timed flood-and-drain cycles
  • Real-time water chemistry across all tanks
  • Closed-loop recycling — uses 100% less water than soil farming
  • Zero pesticides. Zero synthetic fertilisers. Full traceability.

02 — Intelligence

The AI layer is coming.

We are installing an end-to-end sensor network across every tank and grow bed. Every variable measured. Every pattern learned. Every decision data-driven.

Proprietary sensor network · Edge computing clusters · Custom AI pipelines

⬡

pH Precision

Proprietary probes measure water chemistry 24/7. Automated dosing corrects drift before fish or plants are affected.

◈

Dissolved Oxygen

Real-time DO sensors ensure peak saturation across all tanks. Alerts fire in seconds, not hours.

◉

Nutrient Mapping

EC and ORP sensors track nutrient density across every grow bed. AI schedules feeding for maximum plant uptake.

⬟

Climate Control

Temperature, humidity and light sensors across the facility. Every variable logged, every anomaly flagged instantly.

◈

Computer Vision

Camera arrays detect plant stress, fish behaviour anomalies, and pest incursions automatically.

⬡

Predictive Models

AI forecasts harvest windows, equipment failure probabilities, and yield potential weeks in advance.
